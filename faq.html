Ten things customers ask us before they buy a policy or apply for a loan.

What does Armaan FinMitra do?

We are an insurance and loan advisory based in South Dum Dum, Kolkata. We help with 10+ insurance types (health, motor, life, home, fire, marine, CPM, WC, travel) and 11 loan categories, plus claim assistance and policy renewal.

Do you charge for advice?

No. Consultation and guidance are free. You pay only the insurer's premium or the lender's interest.

Can you help with a claim?

Yes. We assist with paperwork, submission and follow-up with the insurer until settlement.

Which documents do I need for health insurance?

Aadhaar, PAN, a photograph and your medical history. For motor insurance: RC, driving licence, previous policy and vehicle photos.

Do you help with loan approvals?

Yes. We match your profile to the right bank or NBFC across personal, business, home, loan against property, gold, car, education and machinery loans.

Can you renew my existing policy?

Yes, any insurer's policy. We send renewal reminders so your cover never lapses.

Do you handle workmen's compensation (WC) and CPM cover?

Yes, for factories, contractors and construction sites.

Do you serve customers outside Kolkata?

Yes. We work with families and businesses across West Bengal and pan-India, online.
