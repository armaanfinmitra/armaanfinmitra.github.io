Questions

Frequently asked

The things people ask us most often.

Do you charge for advice?

No. Consultation, comparison and guidance are completely free. We are paid by the insurer or lender, not by you.

Which areas do you serve?

All of Bharat. Most of the process can be completed online or over the phone.

How long does a loan take?

Gold and personal loans can be approved the same day. Home and property loans usually take 7 to 15 working days, depending on the lender.

Can you help if my claim was rejected?

Yes. Send us the rejection letter and the policy copy. We review it and tell you honestly whether it is worth appealing.

What documents do I need?

Usually Aadhaar, PAN, address proof and income proof. Each insurance entry above lists the exact documents.

My policy has lapsed. Can it be revived?

In most cases yes, if it is within the revival window. Call us before you buy a new policy — reviving is usually cheaper.
